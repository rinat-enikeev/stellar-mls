Onym is a private group chat app where every message is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your device. Built on open protocols, Onym gives you verifiable privacy without trusting a central server.

<b>End-to-End Encryption</b>
Every message is encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device. Only group members can read messages. Not even relay operators can see your conversations.

<b>Cryptographic Group Membership</b>
Group membership is verified using zero-knowledge proofs anchored on the Stellar blockchain. You can prove you belong to a group without revealing who else is in it.

<b>Decentralized Communication</b>
Messages travel through Nostr relays, an open protocol with no single point of failure. Your conversations are not locked into any company's servers.

<b>Push Notifications</b>
Never miss a message. Notifications are end-to-end encrypted with per-subscription keys and decrypted on-device. The push relay uses ephemeral X25519 keys and never learns your identity.

<b>Private Invitations</b>
Invite members via QR codes or invite links. Invitations are encrypted end-to-end so only the intended recipient can join.

<b>Voice Messages</b>
Record and send encrypted voice messages directly in your chats.

<b>Media Sharing</b>
Share photos and images with the same end-to-end encryption applied to all media.

<b>Cross-Platform</b>
Onym works across iOS and Android. Both platforms use identical encryption and wire formats, so you can chat with anyone regardless of their device.

<b>No Phone Number Required</b>
Your identity is a cryptographic key pair generated on your device. No phone number, no email, no personal information needed to start chatting.

<b>Open Source</b>
Onym is built on open standards: Nostr for messaging, Stellar for on-chain membership verification, and standard cryptographic primitives you can audit.
