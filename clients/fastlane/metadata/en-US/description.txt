Onym is a private group chat app where every message is end-to-end encrypted before it leaves your device. Built on open protocols, Onym gives you verifiable privacy by design.

KEY FEATURES

End-to-End Encryption
Every message is encrypted with AES-256-GCM on your device. Only group members can read messages.

Cryptographic Group Membership
Group membership is verified using zero-knowledge proofs anchored on the Stellar blockchain. You can prove you belong to a group without revealing who else is in it.

Decentralized Communication
Messages travel through Nostr relays, an open protocol with no single point of failure and built-in redundancy.

Private Invitations
Invite members via QR codes or invite links. Invitations are encrypted end-to-end so only the intended recipient can join.

Voice Messages
Record and send encrypted voice messages directly in your chats.

Media Sharing
Share photos and images with the same end-to-end encryption applied to all media.

Voice and Video Calls
Make encrypted voice and video calls to your group members.

Contacts
Manage your contacts and see who is available to chat.

Cross-Platform
Onym works across iOS and Android. Both platforms use identical encryption and wire formats, so you can chat with anyone regardless of their device.

Quick Setup
Get started in seconds with no account registration. Your identity is a cryptographic key pair generated on your device.

Open Source
Onym is built on open standards: Nostr (NIP) for messaging, Stellar (SEP) for on-chain membership verification, and standard cryptographic primitives you can audit.
