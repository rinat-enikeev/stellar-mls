Help make Onym's proofs trustworthy on mainnet

Onym's zero-knowledge membership proofs rely on a one-time "trusted setup" — a short ritual that produces the proving keys every Onym client uses. The keys shipping today were generated by a single party, which is why mainnet is still gated. A public multi-party ceremony fixes that: as long as even one participant destroys their contribution, the keys are sound. Running it in the open, with as many diverse contributors as we can gather, is what turns Onym's privacy story from "trust us" into "verify for yourself."

  • Stronger privacy for every Onym user. The 1-of-N honest assumption collapses from "trust one team" to "trust one stranger out of hundreds" — exponentially harder to compromise.
  • A transcript anyone can audit, forever. Every round is published to Nostr and Blossom. You can re-verify the six pairing equations in your browser, today or in ten years.
  • Your contribution is permanent. Participants are recorded by Nostr pubkey in the public ceremony; your npub stays in the transcript as part of what made mainnet possible.
  • No cryptography background required. The tool is a signed native binary; the ceremony UI walks you through every step in plain language.

How it works

Privacy by cryptography, not policy

🔒

Zero-Knowledge Proofs

Groth16 proofs let you prove group membership without revealing your identity. The blockchain only stores opaque cryptographic commitments — never names, keys, or member lists.

⭐

Stellar Blockchain

Group state is anchored on Stellar via a Soroban smart contract. A fee-paying relayer means participants never need funded accounts — just create, join, and chat.

🔐

End-to-End Encrypted

All messages are AES-256-GCM encrypted and transported over Nostr. Relay servers see only ciphertext and topic tags. Media files use content-addressed Blossom storage.

Features

What makes Onym different

No identity required

No phone number, no email, no account. Your membership is a cryptographic proof.

Cross-platform

Native iOS and Android apps with full feature parity.

Constant-size proofs

A 2,048-member group costs the same to verify as a 2-member group.

Media sharing

Images, video, and voice messages via Blossom content-addressed storage.

Push notifications

Never miss a message. Notifications are end-to-end encrypted with per-subscription keys and decrypted on-device. The relay uses ephemeral X25519 keys — it never learns your identity.

Voice & video calls

Encrypted peer-to-peer calls via WebRTC. Connect directly with group members.

On-chain verification

Anyone can verify group state on Stellar — the contract is open and auditable.

Self-hostable

Run your own relay, Nostr server, and Blossom storage. Full sovereignty.
