\section{Benchmark harness}
\label{app:bench}

The first-party measurements in row C1 of
\S\ref{sec:analysis} are produced by the harness at
\texttt{benchmarks/c1/} in this paper's source tree. \texttt{methodology.md} documents the measurement contract
(what is and is not timed; hardware and software pinning;
\texttt{results.json} schema). \texttt{run.sh} executes the harness
and writes \texttt{results.json}, which the table cells in
Tables~\ref{tab:cost}--\ref{tab:pq} consume via generated
\texttt{.tex} snippets. Per-call sampling instrumentation is
deferred from this draft pending a dedicated benchmark
binary; the current harness reports a coarse
\texttt{wall\_clock\_ms\_total} only.
